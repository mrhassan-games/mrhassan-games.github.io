Sky Hopper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Sky Hopper is a single-player game. We respect your privacy and the privacy of children who play it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best score) and settings are saved only on your own device using the browser's local storage. This data never leaves the device, and you can delete it anytime with "Reset progress" in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

The game works offline. On first launch it may download a free font from Google Fonts; this request does not include any personal information from us.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
