Iron Hawk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Iron Hawk is a single-player game by Mr. Hassan Games.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking in this version.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (best times) and settings are saved only on your own device using the browser's local storage. This data never leaves the device, and you can delete it anytime by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

The game works offline. On first launch it may download a free font from Google Fonts; this request does not include any personal information from us.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
